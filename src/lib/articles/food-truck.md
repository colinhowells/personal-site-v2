Some friends had a dream for a food truck, specializing in pickled vegetables; they were incredibly talented cooks, so I was thrilled for them and happy to help out. Like a lot of small businesses it was a labor of love which folded after a struggle, but while it lasted it was terrific.

<Figure src={truck} alt="Food truck" width="2048" height="1536" />

I worked on the logo (they had something extremely specific in mind, so rather than a brainstorming challenge it was a technical one), decals, business cards, and their menu. And I ate very well!
